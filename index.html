About me

I'm ATER at CEREMADE. My research explores how deep learning can be leveraged to model and understand complex physical phenomena.

I received my PhD from Université Paris Dauphine–PSL under the supervision of Boris Haspot and Raphaël Danchin, with funding from the MathInParis2020 doctoral program of the Fondation Sciences Mathématiques de Paris. I previously earned a master's degree in Mathematical Modelling from Sorbonne Université.

Current Topics

My research

Main focus

Physics-Aware Deep Learning

My research investigates the interplay between machine learning and physics, with a focus on modeling spatio-temporal dynamics. I explore three interconnected questions: How can prior physical knowledge be embedded into machine learning models? How can we best leverage both domain expertise and data-driven insights simultaneously? And in what ways can ML models meaningfully complement classical physical approaches?

Secondary focus

Foundation Models for Multiphysics

Beyond physics-informed learning, I am currently working on domain generalization for multi-physics systems and investigating the potential of foundation models to unify learning across diverse physical regimes — with a particular interest in algorithms suitable for large-scale deployment.

Collaboration

Supervision

This project is being carried out under the supervision of Patrick Gallinari at ISIR, Sorbonne University.
